Editorial and privacy rule

Describe it accurately

Call it a free public education and first-pass signal resource. Do not describe it as a bank, government service, police service, investigation, or guarantee.

Use redacted examples

Replace names, phone numbers, account details, codes, addresses, payment information, and live links with labels such as [NAME], [PHONE], [CODE], and [LINK].

Point to official help

For money, account access, identity, or device security, tell readers to use the real organization’s official app, website, or known contact route.

Frequently asked questions

Who can use this publisher kit?

Publishers, NGOs, educators, newsletters, creators, moderators, help centres, and community leaders can use the descriptions and public links.

What should a publisher avoid?

Do not publish private messages, passwords, verification codes, payment details, identity documents, private screenshots, session URLs, or live suspicious links.

Is MindDividend Shield an official investigation service?

No. It is a free public education and first-pass signal resource, not a bank, platform, government agency, investigator, lawyer, or guarantee.
