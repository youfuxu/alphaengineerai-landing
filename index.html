Global scam checker FAQ

What should I do before checking a suspicious message?
Pause before clicking, paying, downloading, replying, sharing a code, or granting remote access. Redact private information first.

Is MindDividend Shield an official investigation or guarantee?
No. It is a free first-pass signal tool. Confirm important claims through the organization’s official website, app, or known contact channel.

What information can I safely paste?
Use only a redacted example without names, phone numbers, addresses, passwords, payment details, verification codes, identity documents, or private account information.
