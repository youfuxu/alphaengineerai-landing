# Before You Click: A Free Public Scam-Safety Reminder

Use this short reminder before clicking a link, paying, replying, downloading a file, or sharing a verification code.

1. **Pause.** Urgency, authority, secrecy, and fear are common pressure tactics.
2. **Do not use the message's link or phone number.** Open the official app or website independently.
3. **Redact examples.** Never share passwords, verification codes, payment details, identity documents, private addresses, wallet keys, or private screenshots.
4. **Verify the request.** Contact the bank, platform, employer, delivery company, or person through a contact route you already know.
5. **If you already acted, move quickly.** Contact the relevant bank or provider, secure the account from an independent device, preserve evidence safely, and use an official reporting route.
