MindDividend Shield

Use a second pair of eyes. Protect the first pair.

MindDividend Shield is designed for a cautious first look at suspicious messages. It is not a bank, police service, lawyer, or emergency response line.

Never paste these details

Account access

Passwords, passkeys, recovery codes, one-time passwords, security questions, and private login links.

Payment information

Full card numbers, security codes, bank account numbers, wallet seed phrases, and payment receipts containing private data.

Identity documents

Passport or identity-card numbers, tax identifiers, full addresses, signatures, and images of official documents.

Private conversations

Names, phone numbers, medical details, intimate images, work secrets, or screenshots that identify another person.

Safer redaction: replace private details with labels such as [BANK], [NAME], [LINK], [PHONE], or [CODE] before using a non-sensitive example.

What the first check can do

Organize visible warning signs

Pressure, impersonation, payment requests, suspicious links, secrecy, and unrealistic promises can be easier to see when listed clearly.

Give you a pause

The best outcome is often simple: you slow down long enough to verify through an official channel.

Suggest safer next steps

If the message involves money, an account, or a device, the guidance points you back to the bank, service, or provider you already trust.

What it cannot do

No guarantee

A first-pass signal cannot prove that a message is authentic or fraudulent. Treat uncertainty as a reason to verify.

No emergency response

If money, identity, or account access is at risk, contact the relevant bank, platform, provider, or local authority immediately.

No private investigation

The tool does not replace a cybersecurity professional, legal advice, fraud investigator, or the official support team.

No contact through the scam

Never call a number, open a link, or reply using details supplied by the suspicious message itself.

How this website is measured

We do not send message text, form input, passwords, verification codes, payment details, or other user-supplied content to Analytics.
