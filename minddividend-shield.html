Scams move quickly. Your first check should be simple.

Scammers use urgency, authority, fear, and attractive offers across every language and platform. Shield turns those familiar pressure tactics into a short, understandable review.

01 / PRESSURE

Urgency signals

Look for countdowns, threats, account-lock warnings, or requests that make you act before you think.

02 / MONEY

Payment risk

Flag gift cards, crypto, wire transfers, unusual fees, and requests for credentials or one-time codes.

03 / IDENTITY

Impersonation clues

Compare the sender, link, request, and story. A familiar name does not prove a message is authentic.

What to do after a warning

  • Do not click, pay, or share a code.
  • Contact the person or company through a known official channel.
  • Report and block the account or message.
  • If money or credentials were shared, contact your bank or service immediately.

Common questions

Is MindDividend Shield a guarantee?

No. It is a practical first-pass signal, not a bank, police, legal, or cybersecurity determination. Confirm important requests independently.

What messages can I check?

Suspicious texts, emails, social DMs, payment requests, account alerts, job offers, delivery notices, and other messages that ask you to click, pay, or disclose information.

Who is it for?

Anyone who wants a quick second look — including families, older adults, creators, freelancers, and small teams dealing with online messages every day.
